## Responsibilities

### Core Product Engineering
* **Development Leadership:** Lead technical development for the core Anatomical Pathology reporting suite, handling end-to-end design and implementation for critical, high-complexity diagnostic modules used by public and private healthcare providers.
    * **Frontend Architecture:** Maintain and evolve a high-performance Angular monorepo with several complex libraries and applications, ensuring modularity, scalability, and maintainability.
    * **Reactive Backend Microservices:** Develop scalable, event-driven backend components using Java with Vert.x event loops and Node.js, backed by MongoDB.
* **Architecture & API Design:** Collaborate with the Product Architect to define technical specifications, data models, and RESTful APIs, ensuring low architectural complexity and high system performance to simplify integration with other applications.
* **Third-Party Integration:** Architect and build integration layers connecting backend services to external hospital enterprise systems to consume, aggregate, and display clinical patient data within the client application.
* **AI Integration:** Explore and implement AI flows and harness to automate repetitive tasks, and improve overall system efficiency. This is a newly emerging area of responsibility, with ongoing research and experimentation to identify optimal integration patterns and workflows. The main goal is to leverage AI to facilitate and improve the actual development process.

### Documentation & Delivery Support
* **Documentation & Technical Standards:** Author and maintain comprehensive functional documentation on Confluence, GitHub Wiki for technical content and details, API endpoints for integration, and coding/best practice conventions.
* **Delivery Support & Issue Resolution:** Facilitate technical guidance and troubleshooting for delivery teams during customer installations, upgrades, and edge cases. By reducing the time to resolution for complex issues, I help ensure smooth installations and upgrades avoiding application issues.

### Mentoring & Recruiting
* **Research & Development:** Continuously explore new technologies, frameworks, and best practices to enhance team capabilities and product quality.
* **Internal Training:** Conduct internal workshops and training sessions to disseminate knowledge on new updates, standards, and best practices.
* **Onboarding & Facilitation:** Serve as technical mentor and team facilitator; successfully onboarded several engineers, reducing time-to-autonomy to few months for junior team members.
* **Technical Recruiting:** Conduct technical interviews for engineering candidates, evaluating candidates with a heavy focus on frontend architecture and TypeScript/Angular expertise.
